## Fixed pre-specified test

Compare *signed integer* delta `clamp_s16(floor(0.31 × existing open NAM f64 × 32768)) − original Manager PCM16` against the existing **open candidate output first difference** `0.31 × 32768 × (open[i] − open[i−1])` in the Stage111-defined phases and controls. Also compute derivative RMS of existing candidate DI. No optimization of gain, lag, offset, resampler, model or phase was performed. 

`GT1` means `abs(integer_delta) > 1 LSB`. Slope RMS units are **PCM16 LSB per 48-kHz frame** and are not a proprietary DSP parameter. Rank-based top decile is the **ceil(10%) samples with largest |open first difference|**, with stable tie handling. Pearson is a **descriptive** correlation only, not a parameter fit or a hypothesis test; values are local to each interval. Input is the **candidate resampled DI**, not a verified vendor intermediate.

## Main observations

- **Post-input-zero tail:** After candidate DI ends at absolute frame **1,344,016**, the first 1,000 zero-DI frames contain **392** GT1 errors with candidate output derivative RMS **17.03 LSB/frame**. The next 1,000 zero-DI frames contain only **16** GT1 errors and derivative RMS **2.45 LSB/frame**. Across these 2,000 frames there are **408** GT1s (matching Stage111), but the residual's magnitude/shape does not identify a unique processing cause.
- **Short nonzero-DI pocket:** The fixed `[1,488,000,1,488,200)` interval contains **32** GT1 errors (12 positive and 20 negative), derivative RMS **25.83 LSB/frame** and correlation **0.393** between absolute integer error and absolute open derivative. **9** of its GT1 frames lie in the top **20** derivative frames (10%). Adjacent `[1,487,800,1,488,000)` and `[1,488,200,1,488,400)` have no GT1 at all. Candidate DI is nonzero for all three 200-frame windows.
- **Strong falsification of a simple amplitude/slope threshold:** The two predeclared 2,000-frame Stage111 controls have *far larger* output first-difference RMS, **390.17** and **114.33 LSB/frame**, yet **zero GT1**. Thus large output slope **alone is insufficient** to predict mismatches. Correlations of |error| with |output slope| are only **~0.341** in the first post-zero 1000 and **~0.393** in the secondary event, not mechanistic proof.
- **Additional fixed 16-frame grids:** `[1343960,1345240)` contains **429** GT1 and `[1487984,1488128)` contains **32** GT1. They refine event shape using predetermined windows, not re-estimating historical gain or timing. Full bin details remain in the private report.
